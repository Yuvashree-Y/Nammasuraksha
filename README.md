Namma Suraksha 🚦

A Digital Road Safety & Citizen Reporting Platform

Namma Suraksha is a road safety application designed to connect citizens with authorities and make reporting road-related issues easier and more accessible.

The platform allows citizens to report problems such as damaged roads, unsafe areas, and other road safety concerns using images and location information. It also provides features such as emergency assistance and a rewards-based system to encourage responsible citizen participation.

🎯 Objective

The main objective of Namma Suraksha is to encourage citizens to actively participate in improving road safety by providing a simple digital platform for reporting and addressing road-related issues.

✨ Key Features

- 👤 Citizen Login — Secure access for citizens.
- 🏛️ Authority Login — Dedicated access for responsible authorities.
- 📸 Issue Reporting — Citizens can report road safety problems with photos.
- 📍 Location-Based Reporting — Helps identify where an issue has been reported.
- 🚨 Emergency Assistance — Quick access to emergency support.
- 🏆 Rewards System — Encourages citizens to actively report safety issues.
- 📊 Issue Management — Helps authorities review and respond to reported problems.
- 📱 User-Friendly Interface — Designed with simplicity and accessibility in mind.
